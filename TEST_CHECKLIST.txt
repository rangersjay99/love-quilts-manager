Love Quilts Manager 7.8.66 — sign-in check

1. On a signed-out device, confirm Forgot password? appears below Sign In.
2. Tap it with an empty/invalid email; confirm the app asks for an account email and sends nothing.
3. Enter the approved email and tap Forgot password?; confirm the success instruction appears.
4. Open the reset message in that email inbox, set a new password, and sign in.
5. Close and reopen the app to confirm the sign-in remains saved; verify shared inventory appears.
6. Test with Wi-Fi/cellular off to confirm a helpful connection message.
