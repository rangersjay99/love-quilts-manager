Love Quilts Manager Update 7.8.61
Released: August 8, 2026

Unsaved Entry Protection & One-Size Auto-Fill

Changes:
- Warns before leaving or canceling supported data-entry workflows when entered/changed information has not been saved.
- Uses clear Stay and Finish Entry / Discard & Continue choices inside the app.
- Browser refresh/close also requests the browser's native leave-page confirmation while an entry is dirty.
- Automatically selects a quilt size when the chosen charity has exactly one eligible size.
- Charities with multiple eligible sizes still require an explicit size choice.

No Firebase paths, inventory calculations, needs calculations, reports, or saved transaction/need schemas changed.
