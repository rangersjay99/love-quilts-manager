Love Quilts Manager Update 7.8.46
Released: July 29, 2026

Exact Charity-and-Size Planning Totals update.

Changes:
- Calculates Quilts Still to Be Made using only current inventory that matches the exact charity and quilt size of an open calendar need.
- Keeps quilts in other sizes in their recorded inventory category instead of automatically treating them as substitutes.
- Shows current usable inventory, exact-match inventory, and other unmatched inventory separately so the totals are easy to explain.
- With the July 29 backup: 347 remaining calendar need - 155 exact-match inventory = 192 still to make.
- Projected Total Quilts Made by Year End is 282 made so far + 192 still to make = 474.
- Continues excluding Unassigned/Storage test activity from official Made, Distributed, Available in Storage, yearly, and lifetime totals.
- Deleted transactions remain excluded because they are removed from the active data arrays.
- Retains the iPhone/iPad printing and Custom Reports improvements introduced in Update 7.8.43.

Firebase production paths and the shared-data schema are unchanged.
