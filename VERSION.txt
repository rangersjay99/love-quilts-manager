Love Quilts Manager Update 7.8.44
Released: July 29, 2026

Year-End Quilt Production Projection update.

Changes:
- Makes Projected Total Quilts Made by Year End the main Yearly Quilt Summary number.
- Calculates the projection as Quilts Made So Far plus Quilts Still to Be Made.
- Keeps Quilts Distributed separate so it is not added to the production projection.
- Updates Full, Compact, meeting, and Custom Newsletter reports with the corrected wording and formula.
- Retains the iPhone/iPad printing and Custom Reports improvements introduced in Update 7.8.43.

Firebase production paths and the shared-data schema are unchanged.
