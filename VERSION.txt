Love Quilts Manager Update 7.8.45
Released: July 29, 2026

Pooled Inventory Planning Totals update.

Changes:
- Counts current usable inventory pooled across sizes within each charity toward the remaining calendar need, regardless of the size currently assigned within that charity.
- Calculates Still to Be Made as Remaining Calendar Need minus Current Usable Inventory.
- Keeps exact charity/size detail on calendar rows so coordinators can still see where swaps may be needed.
- Excludes Unassigned/Storage test activity from official Made, Distributed, Available in Storage, and yearly totals.
- Keeps deleted records excluded because deleted transactions are not present in the active data arrays.
- Retains the iPhone/iPad printing and Custom Reports improvements introduced in Update 7.8.43.

Firebase production paths and the shared-data schema are unchanged.
