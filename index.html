Change Log

Current version: Update 7.8.58Coordinated Report Design · August 5, 2026

7.8.58

Standardizes the Home report snapshot, Reports screen, previews, print layouts, and PDF headings with a coordinated plum-branded header, clear report title and date block, consistent section headings, tables, totals, spacing, and footer styling. Report calculations and one-tap navigation are unchanged.

7.8.57

Adds a synchronized Header Tagline setting under Names, Wording & Tabs. The line beneath the app title can now be customized without changing reports, inventory, needs, or calculations.

7.8.56

Restores the original plum-purple branding on the splash screen and page headers, reduces blue across normal cards and reports, and keeps red reserved for genuine shortages, errors, and warnings. Calendar clarity improvements from 7.8.55 remain unchanged.

7.8.55

Reserves red and pink for shortages, errors, and destructive warnings. Normal screens now use teal, blue, sage, and neutral backgrounds. The current Calendar month has a stronger teal frame, highlighted header, CURRENT MONTH badge, and a quick jump button. Short quantities are larger and bold.

7.8.54

Simplifies Create Need & Distribute into a clear sequence: Quilts Received, Total Quilts Needed, and Quilts to Distribute Now, followed by Continue. The distributed amount can differ from the received amount; any difference is correctly added to or taken from matching charity-and-size storage.

7.8.53

Add Quilts can now create a charity need and immediately distribute the incoming quilts in one protected save. The Calendar shows the full need, quantity sent, and balance still needed. Review screens keep Confirm & Save visible, show NOT SAVED YET, warn before leaving, and display a clear success banner only after saving.

7.8.52

Fixes the shared-device sync indicator introduced by 7.8.51. Firebase now treats the older misspelled Unassigned/Storage label and the corrected label as the same category, allowing every device to complete verification without changing inventory totals or deleting records.

7.8.51

Adds a selected-year Quilts Distributed by Charity — YTD breakdown using the actual distribution activity date, with charity totals and an overall total. Add Quilts now also includes a No current charity need option that stores the entry under Unassigned/Storage without changing existing inventory, planning, or Firebase data rules.

7.8.50

Replaces every native iPhone print-dialog action with the same direct PDF-opening method already proven to work on iPhone. Home Summary, Calendar, Full, Compact, Custom, and Audit reports now open PDFs for printing without changing any totals or records.

7.8.49

Removes the four historical deleted Unassigned/Storage test records from every operational total and retains them only in Audit History. Active Unassigned/Storage inventory remains countable, while an unlinked storage removal cannot be counted as a charity distribution.

7.8.48

Changes the blank inventory-and-needs worksheet to a reliable printable PDF and integrates the custom logo beside the title so it displays consistently on every main tab.

7.8.47

Removes the remaining asynchronous iPhone print delay, counts active Unassigned/Storage records, keeps deleted records out of every operational total while retaining an Audit History, adds a printable blank inventory-and-needs worksheet, and adds a synchronized custom logo.

7.8.46

Uses only inventory matching the exact charity and quilt size when calculating Quilts Still to Be Made. Other usable inventory remains in storage and is shown separately, but it does not automatically reduce demand for another size.

7.8.45

Counts current usable inventory pooled across sizes within each charity toward the remaining calendar need because the guild may swap sizes within a charity. Unassigned/Storage test activity is excluded from official made, distributed, storage, and yearly totals.

7.8.44

Updated the Yearly Quilt Summary to clearly show the guild’s projected total quilts made by year end. The projection equals Quilts Made So Far plus Quilts Still to Be Made; Quilts Distributed remains a separate activity total.

7.8.43

Improved iPhone and iPad printing with a prepared print snapshot and added Custom Newsletter Reports with selectable content and saved presets.

7.8.41

Fixed Firebase synchronization for the customizable Future Modules message and bottom navigation labels, including existing customized wording saved before this update.

7.8.40

Added a customizable Future Modules message on the splash and Home screens, plus customizable wording for all five bottom navigation tabs. Icons, colors, order, and destinations remain fixed.

7.8.39

The Reports headline summary cards now match the Home screen cards in size, spacing, three-card layout, buttons, and tap behavior.

7.8.38

Reports keeps the three headline totals visible and places Yearly Statistics, Inventory & Quilts Requested, Distributed & Adjustments, and Report Downloads into collapsed tap-to-open sections. Only one report section stays open at a time.

7.8.37

Inventory and Quilts Needed now start with both record sections collapsed for a cleaner, less cluttered screen. Tapping a section opens it, and Home shortcuts or edit actions still open the needed section automatically.

7.8.36

Added tap-to-open sections to Inventory and Quilts Needed. Inventory separates Current Inventory from Inventory History, while Quilts Needed separates active requests from completed Needs History. Only one section stays open at a time.

7.8.35

Made the splash growth message open Future Modules, grouped Settings into short tap-to-open sections, limited Change Log to the newest two entries until Show All is selected, and removed the duplicate Calendar from Quilts Needed. The Home Calendar remains the single calendar view.

7.8.34

Added a display-only Future Modules section in Settings with five possible expansion areas. Added the subtle splash-screen line “Designed to grow with your quilting needs.” No module functions, data fields, or Firebase changes were added.

7.8.33

Added Edit Date beside distributed records in Reports, with linked inventory and yearly statistics following the revised date. Added a Settings tool that checks common data problems and creates structured bug reports or change requests for sharing, copying, or downloading.

7.8.32

Reports now use the selected date when inventory changed or quilts were distributed, including backdated distribution edits and corrections. Entry timestamps remain available for audit history.

7.8.31

Fixed the blank Compact Report print preview and restored Compact One-Page PDF downloads.

7.8.30

Added safe charity and quilt-size renaming that preserves all existing records and numbers. Replaced old inventory edit/delete and charity-need edit/distribution/delete pop-ups with the detailed Home-style Before/After verification screen.

7.8.29

Moved Add Quilts Needed to the top of the Needs tab, colored changed request totals green or red, and changed the separate distribution reminder to purple.

7.8.28

Changed unchanged verification bubbles to a calm gray so they do not appear to signal a problem.

7.8.27

Separated Add Quilts Needed from distribution and moved familiar Home-style Before/After bubbles to the top of verification.

7.8.26

Moved Quilts Needed to the top, returned to Home after new additions, and added exact Before/After Home totals to verification.

7.8.25

Fixed a green status appearing before the server copy was confirmed. Sync Now now sends the correct over-distribution repair from the device that has it, while a second device safely receives the latest shared copy instead of uploading stale data.

7.8.24

Removed the remaining Firebase cap on distributed quantities, repaired clipped 7.8.23 quantities when linked inventory confirmed the full amount, and rewrote corrected need records.

7.8.23
