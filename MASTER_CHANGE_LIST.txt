LOVE QUILTS MANAGER — MASTER CHANGE LIST
Updated July 25, 2026

IMPLEMENTED THROUGH UPDATE 7.8.27
- Update 7.8.27 separates Add Quilts Needed from distribution and moves color-coded Home-style Before/After bubbles to the top of verification.
- Update 7.8.26 moves Quilts Needed to the top, returns to Home after new additions, and adds exact Home Before/After verification bubbles.

HOME
- At a Glance provides Add Quilts, Add Need, and Distribute Quilts actions.
- Calendar appears immediately after At a Glance.
- This Year shows quilts made and distributed.
- By Charity shows storage, requests, and quilts still to complete.

CALENDAR
- Past demand met is light green.
- Past demand not met is light red.
- Ordinary future months are light yellow.
- Future months covered early are light green.
- Current month has a bold plum border.
- Charity sections are green when met and light red when short.
- Tapping a charity entry offers Edit Need, Distribute Quilts, and View Details.
- Calendar distribution prefills charity, size, month, quantity, and date fields.
- The color key is at the bottom of each Calendar.
- Repeated Add Quilts Needed buttons are removed from month cards.
- No duplicate instruction appears above the Home Calendar.
- Each populated month card says “Tap charity to edit” at the bottom.
- The 12-month printout uses portrait orientation in a 3 × 4 layout.

REPORTS AND STATISTICS
- Report wording matches Home wording.
- The third Reports summary block displays fully on iPhone.
- Alternating row shading is used on screen and in output.
- Yearly and lifetime statistics are included.
- Full, compact, Summary, Calendar, CSV, backup, print, and email/share tools remain.

INVENTORY, DISTRIBUTION, AND SYNC
- Every new inventory entry requires a review screen showing the entered details and resulting inventory totals before saving.
- Every new charity need/request requires a review screen with Home-style Before/After bubbles at the top, followed by entered details and resulting request totals.
- Adding a charity need is separate from distribution; distributions are entered later through Mark Distributed or the Calendar action.
- Quantity Distributed may exceed the original request; the full quantity is recorded and reported while Quilts Still Needed remains zero.
- Set Current Count accepts exact counts, including zero.
- A full distribution immediately reduces active inventory to zero when appropriate.
- Duplicate deductions are prevented.
- Corrected distribution quantities safely restore or deduct only the difference.
- Linked distribution transactions remain protected.
- Sync Now is available in the status banner and Settings.
- Update 7.8.24 removed the Firebase quantity cap and repaired affected 7.8.23 synchronized quantities.
- Update 7.8.25 makes Sync Now direction-safe across two devices and requires server confirmation before showing green.
- Firebase shared-device paths and existing data remain compatible.

DEFERRED / FUTURE ROADMAP
- On Hold / Storage workflow is removed from the active app for now; preserve the idea for a later optional workflow.
- Optional first-of-month reminder emails remain lower priority.
- Custom logo and guided first-use setup remain future customization work.
- Photo support, standalone edition, Quilt Show Manager, and other optional modules remain long-term items.
- Hold Version 8 for a later major release.
